# Experiment catalog and current status

The latest completed experiment is the [five-agent full study v2](FULL_STUDY_RESULTS_V2.md), with 3,500 audited generations across development, validation and held-out test. It retains the selected original calculator prompt, strict schema and driver-621.24 environment. The [architecture pilots](ARCHITECTURE_RESULTS_V1.md), [twelve-question confirmation](CONFIRMATION_RESULTS_V1.md), earlier [offline comparisons](OFFLINE_POLICY_RESULTS_V1.md), and [communication-pilot recovery](COMMUNICATION_RESULTS_V2.md) remain separate development evidence. Historical scores use different contracts and must not be combined into one model leaderboard.

The earlier confirmation is complete and audited: all 840 generations and evaluations, source/input hashes, canonical requests and deterministic reports reproduce. Its first preflight stopped before inference because the NVIDIA driver changed from 617.42 to 621.24. The [confirmation environment](../config/confirmation_environment.json) records the prospective driver-only amendment; both branches shared the new pinned environment. Validation/test were excluded at that development stage and have now been generated in the full study. See the [earlier results](CONFIRMATION_RESULTS_V1.md).

Both [architecture pilots](ARCHITECTURE_PILOTS.md) are complete and audited: 420 five-agent calls and 840 sparse ten-agent calls, with canonical routing and all report bytes reproduced. These are fresh-seed replications on twelve inspected development questions, not new holdout evidence. No offline subset screen preceded them. The [result interpretation](ARCHITECTURE_RESULTS_V1.md) records the full-study recommendation; the reports themselves perform no automatic architecture selection.

The [five-agent full study v2](FULL_EXPERIMENT.md) has completed and passed independent replay of all 3,500 calls, source identities, phase dependencies and report bytes. Development calibrated request-time/token cost; validation selected R0 before test, giving an identical shared-initial primary test result of 12/40 in each branch. Later-round curves are secondary results and did not retune the selection. [All 100 prompts](FULL_STUDY_QUESTIONS.md) and the former ten-agent v1 design remain preserved.

All generations in these records used either Qwen2.5:3b or Llama3.2:3b. [Model identity and weight availability](MODEL_REGISTRY.md) are tracked separately from prompt/transport/scoring profiles. No evidence of another tested model family was found in the available records.

The [archive index](../archive/README.md) covers nine dated snapshots and the separate available-model backup. The newest [full-study snapshot](../archive/2026-11-19-full-study-v2/README.md) preserves 29 originals: twenty-one study/artifact/phase evidence files and eight new frozen source/configuration/launcher files. Its inventory maps the other 41 manifest-bound inputs to exact copies in prior archives. Older snapshots remain unchanged. Operational originals remain where their absolute provenance expects them and where historical imports and audits depend on those paths.

The eight Stage-A2 screening questions are numerical variants of inspected development structures, not structural holdout. Regression and screening share semantic groups. Validation/test questions were excluded from pilot inference; their full-study outcomes now evaluate the separately frozen procedure. Pilot results did not select a validated optimal communication round; full-study validation performed its prespecified selection before test.
